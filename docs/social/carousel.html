The idea

A skill is a folder
your agent loads on its own.

Instructions plus scripts. You don't call it by name: you say "add my app to awesome-mac" and the right one loads.

skills/launch-lists/
├── SKILL.md # rules and steps
└── scripts/
    ├── insert-list-line.py
    └── add-json-entry.py
